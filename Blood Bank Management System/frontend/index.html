📝 Register New Donor

Fill in the donor's details. They'll be added to the system and eligible for future donations.

💡 Quick Tips

📋Donors must be 18–65 years old to be eligible.
🕐A minimum 90-day gap between donations is enforced.
🆔Each donor gets a unique ID for tracking.
📱Phone numbers help recipients connect with donors.

💉 Record a Donation

Enter the donor's ID (visible in Admin panel). Eligibility is verified automatically.
